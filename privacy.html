1. Who we are

ZigZagTroupe Daily Drop (the "App") is operated by Troupe UniverZe / ZigZagTroupe ("Troupe", "we", "us"), a content-creator organization with a social media presence, including the TikTok account @zigzagtroupe. We are not a data broker. The App is an internal tool used to publish Troupe's own videos to Troupe's own TikTok account, currently in trial / sandbox use through TikTok's official Login Kit (OAuth) and Content Posting API.

2. What data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile (scope user.info.basic): the authorizing account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Video publishing (scopes video.upload, video.publish): permission to upload and publish videos to the authorizing account. We send our own videos to TikTok; we do not read other users' content, followers, messages or analytics.
  • OAuth tokens: the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues after authorization.
  • Video files: our own video files, which the App ingests from Troupe's own Google Drive.

We do not collect data from the public, and we do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on these pages.

3. How we use it

Only to post on the authorized account's behalf when authorized: preparing Troupe's videos and uploading or publishing them to the connected TikTok account, and confirming which account is connected.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored privately on Troupe's own operator computer, in a restricted secrets store, and are not published, shared or committed to any repository. Video files stay in Troupe's own Google Drive and on the operator's computer while being processed.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not share it with third parties, except with TikTok itself as needed to publish videos through its API, or where required by law.

6. Retention

Tokens are kept only while the App is authorized and are deleted when access is revoked or on request. Basic profile details are kept only as long as the connection exists.

7. Your choices: access, return and deletion

You can request a copy of, or deletion of, your data at any time. See Data Return / Deletion. You can also revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with a new effective date.
