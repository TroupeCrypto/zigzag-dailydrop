2. What the App does

The App is a single-operator, internal tool. It takes finished video files from Troupe's own Google Drive, prepares them (for example, trimming and formatting for vertical video), and uploads or publishes them to a TikTok account that has authorized the App through TikTok's official Login Kit (OAuth) and Content Posting API.
