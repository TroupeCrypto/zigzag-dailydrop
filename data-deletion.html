ZigZagTroupe Daily Drop is operated by Troupe UniverZe / ZigZagTroupe. If you have authorized the App on TikTok, you can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to delete it.

How to make a request

  1. Email zigzagtroupe@gmail.com with the subject "Daily Drop data request".
  2. Say whether you want a copy of your data, deletion, or both, and include your TikTok username so we can find the right record.

What happens next

  • We handle requests within 30 days.
  • Copy (data return): we email you the data we hold for your account (basic profile details and a record of the connection; tokens themselves are never sent by email).
  • Deletion: we revoke the App's TikTok authorization, delete the stored access and refresh tokens and any stored profile details, and confirm by email when done.

Revoke access yourself

You can remove the App's access at any time in the TikTok app's settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions, or the equivalent in your version of TikTok). Once access is revoked, the App can no longer use your account.
